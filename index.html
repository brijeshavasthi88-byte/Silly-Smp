Server Rules

Keep it silly, keep it kind.

  • 1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech or toxicity.
  • 2. No griefing or stealing. Protect your builds with claims and respect others'.
  • 3. No cheating. Hacked clients, x-ray and exploits are not allowed.
  • 4. No spam or advertising. Keep chat clean and friendly.
  • 5. Listen to staff. Their decisions keep the server fun for everyone.
